### Executive Summary

Phase 11 introduces LuminaLearn's production-oriented Vietnamese AI narration, audio synchronization, and subtitle subsystem without compromising the platform's strict mathematical verification, teacher approval barriers, artifact immutability, or multi-tenant isolation.

Prior to Phase 11, narration audio was concatenated as an uncalibrated monolithic string, resulting in audio duration overflow, subtitle timing drift, silent truncation, and lack of per-scene speech caching. Phase 11 implements:

1. **Vietnamese Narration Normalizer (`vietnamese-narration-normalizer.ts`)**: Rule-based normalizer converting mathematical formulas (algebraic identities, fractions, powers, derivatives, integrals) and AI/ML domain concepts into natural, phonetically correct Vietnamese text suitable for TTS engines.
2. **Content-Addressed Audio Cache Manager (`audio-cache-manager.ts`)**: Content/voice-derived SHA-256 caching with atomic filesystem transactions, restart recovery, quota management (LRU eviction), and multi-tenant teacher isolation.
3. **Scene-Level Audio Synchronization Engine (`scene-audio-synchronizer.ts`)**: Frame-accurate integer timeline reconciliation (24 FPS default), configurable pre-roll and post-roll padding, dynamic scene presentation extension (preventing silent speech truncation), and FFmpeg-based multi-input concat multiplexing.
4. **Subtitles & SRT Alignment (`subtitles.ts`)**: Dual-delivery support (burned-in video subtitles and independent downloadable `.srt`), strictly aligned to actual speech boundaries with zero time-cursor drift.
5. **Teacher Studio Audio Capabilities (`SceneInspectorV3.tsx` & `/api/v3/lessons/[lessonId]/preview-audio`)**: Direct per-scene audio previewing, character and word count inspection, normalized text display, cache hit monitoring, and downloadable subtitles.

### 3. Vietnamese Narration Normalization Rules

The normalizer transforms raw mathematical symbols and AI jargon into natural spoken Vietnamese:

#### A. Mathematical Expressions & Identities
- `(a + b)^2` $\rightarrow$ "a cộng b tất cả bình phương"
- `(a - b)^2` $\rightarrow$ "a trừ b tất cả bình phương"
- `a^2 - b^2` $\rightarrow$ "a bình phương trừ b bình phương"
- `(a + b)^3` $\rightarrow$ "a cộng b tất cả lập phương"
- `(a - b)^3` $\rightarrow$ "a trừ b tất cả lập phương"
- `a^3 + b^3` $\rightarrow$ "a lập phương cộng b lập phương"
- `a^3 - b^3` $\rightarrow$ "a lập phương trừ b lập phương"
- `a^2 + 2ab + b^2` $\rightarrow$ "a bình phương cộng hai a b cộng b bình phương"
- `a^2 - 2ab + b^2` $\rightarrow$ "a bình phương trừ hai a b cộng b bình phương"
- Fractions: `\frac{1}{2}` $\rightarrow$ "1 phần 2"
- Powers: `x^2` $\rightarrow$ "x bình phương", `x^3` $\rightarrow$ "x lập phương", `x^n` $\rightarrow$ "x mũ n"
- Derivatives: `f'(x)` $\rightarrow$ "f phẩy x", `\frac{df}{dx}` $\rightarrow$ "đạo hàm của f theo x"
- Integrals: `\int_0^1 x^2 dx` $\rightarrow$ "tích phân từ không đến một của x bình phương theo x"
- Subscripts: `w_{t+1}` $\rightarrow$ "w tại bước t cộng một", `w_t` $\rightarrow$ "w tại bước t", `w_1` $\rightarrow$ "w một"

#### B. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Terminology
- `Gradient Descent` $\rightarrow$ "phương pháp hạ độ dốc"
- `Perceptron` $\rightarrow$ "mô hình pơ-sép-tròn"
- `Loss function` $\rightarrow$ "hàm mất mát"
- `Learning rate` $\rightarrow$ "tốc độ học"
- `Weight vector` $\rightarrow$ "véc-tơ trọng số"
- `Bias` $\rightarrow$ "độ lệch"
- `Activation function` $\rightarrow$ "hàm kích hoạt"
- `Backpropagation` $\rightarrow$ "thuật toán lan truyền ngược"
- `Self-Attention` $\rightarrow$ "cơ chế tự chú ý"
- `Query, Key, Value` $\rightarrow$ "truy vấn, khóa và giá trị"
- `Convergence` $\rightarrow$ "sự hội tụ"
- `Local minimum` $\rightarrow$ "cực tiểu cục bộ"
- `Global minimum` $\rightarrow$ "cực tiểu toàn cục"

#### C. Numbers, Decimals, Negatives, & Percentages

### 4. Audio Caching & Quota Management

- **Cache Key**: Deterministically computed as `sha256(canonicalJson({ text, providerId, voice, speakingRate, pitch, language }))`. Strictly avoids including API keys or authorization secrets.
- **Atomic Persistence**: Files are written as `.mp3.tmp` and `.meta.json.tmp` before atomic rename to prevent half-written files.
- **SHA-256 Verification**: On every retrieval and restart recovery, the raw audio buffer's SHA-256 hash is computed and compared against the sidecar metadata. Tampered files are immediately evicted.
- **Owner Isolation**: Entries store `ownerId` and refuse cross-teacher read access.
- **LRU Quota Eviction**: Enforces `maxBytes` (default 250MB) and `maxEntries` (default 2000), discarding the least-recently-accessed entries.

### 5. Timeline Reconciliation & Drift Prevention

```
Scene i:
  [--- Pre-roll (0.5s) ---][===== Narration Speech =====][--- Post-roll (0.5s) ---]
  ├─────────────────────── Adjusted Scene Duration ──────────────────────────────┤
  └─ Frame 0              └─ speechStartFrame           └─ speechEndFrame         └─ endFrame
```

1. **Integer Frame Math**: All timings are clamped to exact integer frames at the video's FPS (24 FPS default).
2. **Zero Speech Truncation**: If speech audio duration $D_a + 1.0\text{s} > D_s$ (original target duration), the scene is automatically extended to $D_a + 1.0\text{s}$. Video is never artificially shortened, and audio is never clipped with `-t`.
3. **Padded Audio Concat**: Each scene's audio track is padded with `adelay` and `apad` to exactly equal its adjusted scene presentation duration before multi-input concatenation. This ensures zero drift between video animations and spoken explanations.

### 6. Subtitles Delivery & Formatting

1. **Burned-in Remotion Subtitles**: Rendered through `LuminaSceneV3` and `EducationalPreviewPanelV3` with high-contrast text outlines and safe-area margins.
2. **Downloadable `.srt` File**: Generated alongside `final.mp4` in standard SubRip format with UTF-8 encoding and microsecond timestamp precision (`HH:MM:SS,mmm`).
3. **Frame-Accurate Alignment**: Subtitle cue timestamps match `speechStartFrame` and `speechEndFrame` with exact frame offsets.
